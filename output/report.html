Corroboration - the project's own rule

A claim is called corroborated only when two or more independent sources carry it. In this campaign, 0 claims have no source linked, 19 rest on a single source (so they are not corroborated), 41 have two, and 1 have three or more.

What a score does not mean

  • Not a prediction. A high CEAGI does not mean a party will keep its promises; it means its promises are better evidenced and its record better documented.
  • Not a judgement of the policy. We grade whether a claim can be checked, not whether it is wise, affordable or desirable.
  • Not comparable across different coverage. Where a sub-index is missing it is shown as n/a and never guessed.

A dimension with no evidence is n/a; it is never imputed. A full score is published only when all six exist.

6. Legal basis - what the law actually says

The seat arithmetic in the charts and the simulator comes from Morocco's organic law, not from convention. The text in force is loi organique n° 27.11 on the House of Representatives, consolidated on 29 January 2026 and amended most recently by loi organique n° 53.25 (January 2026), and before that by n° 04.21 (2021) and n° 20.16 (2016). Two articles carry the arithmetic that decides the 395 seats:

What this means in practice

  • 395 seats, two levels. 305 members are elected in local constituencies and 90 in regional constituencies; the law fixes the 90 regional seats in a table across the twelve regions.
  • Proportional, largest remainder. Seats go to lists in proportion to their support, and the seats left over after the quota go to the lists with the largest remainders.
  • The quota is based on registered voters, not votes cast. That is the distinctive Moroccan rule. In practice few lists reach the quota, so most seats are decided at the largest-remainder step.
  • No electoral threshold. The 3%/6% threshold that applied from 2002 to 2016 was removed by the 2021 reform; the current law sets none.
  • The former national list is gone. The 90 seats that used to be elected on a single national list are now elected in the regional constituencies.

The interactive seat simulator on this site applies the largest-remainder method to national vote totals that you enter. It is an illustration at national level: the real count runs constituency by constituency, on the registered-voter quotient. Read it with that in mind.

16. Methodology (short)

Sources to facts/claims to synthesis. A claim is "corroborated" only with 2+ independent reliable sources. Delivery D = (fulfilled + 0.5·partial) / (fulfilled + partial + failed + abandoned), computed over completed terms only, and requires at least 3 concluded promises before it is scored at all. Credibility C = Σ(weight·rubric) / 5Σweight with class weights Q=1.0, V=0.4, S=0.1, N=0.2. Electoral efficiency E = 1 - |A-1| / max|A-1| where A = seat share / vote share for the 2021 election. Aggregation is a weighted geometric mean; uncertainty is 8,000 Monte-Carlo draws at σ=0.05. Full derivation in morocco-elections.org; per-number provenance in output/audit_trail.md.

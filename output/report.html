2. How every grade in this report is decided

Nothing here is a black box. Each claim carries a class (what kind of commitment it is) and a verification score (how checkable it is), both stored in data/claims.csv; each source carries a confidence level and a reliability score. The tables below are the exact rules, and the last column shows how many of this year's 61 campaign claims fall in each bucket.

Corroboration - the project's own rule

A claim is called corroborated only when two or more independent sources carry it. In this campaign, 0 claims have no source linked, 19 rest on a single source (so they are not corroborated), 41 have two, and 1 have three or more.

What a score does not mean

  • Not a prediction. A high CEAGI does not mean a party will keep its promises; it means its promises are better evidenced and its record better documented.
  • Not a judgement of the policy. We grade whether a claim can be checked, not whether it is wise, affordable or desirable.
  • Not comparable across different coverage. Where a sub-index is missing it is shown as n/a and never guessed.

Glossary

CEAGI
Composite Electoral Accountability & Governance Index - this project's six-dimension score. It measures how evidenced a party's promises are and how well documented its record is, not how good its policies are.
Sub-index
One of the six dimensions D, C, E, G, L, M. Each is normalised to 0-1; a dimension with no evidence is shown as n/a.
Claim
A single promise or commitment taken from a party programme - one per row in data/claims.csv.
Quantified (Q)
A claim with a number and a unit, e.g. 'raise the minimum wage to 5,000 dirhams'.
Verification score
A 1-5 grade of how checkable a claim is: 1 = unverifiable, 5 = independently audited.
Corroborated
Carried by at least two independent sources. A claim resting on one source is not corroborated under this project's rule.
Confidence
How solid the sourcing is: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW or ILLUSTRATIVE.
FILL
A cell the registry does not have yet. It is printed, not hidden, so the hole stays visible.
n/a
Not computable from the evidence. It is never replaced by a guess or by zero.
Quotient électoral
The electoral quota. In Moroccan law it is the number of registered voters in a constituency divided by the seats allocated to it.
Plus fort reste
Largest remainder - the rule that assigns the seats left over after the quota to the lists with the largest remainders.
Circonscription
A voting district (constituency). 305 seats are filled in local constituencies and 90 in the twelve regional ones.
AMO
Assurance Maladie Obligatoire - Morocco's compulsory health insurance.
Promise ledger
The record of what parties promised in past terms and what happened (fulfilled, partial, failed, still open).

3. What CEAGI measures

Before the numbers: CEAGI is this project's own yardstick, not an external rating agency. Its definition is written down once, in METHODOLOGY.md, and quoted here in full:

Six things make it up — D delivery of the 2021 promises, C promise credibility, E electoral efficiency, G governance, L leadership, M mandate coherence — and because they are multiplied together, a party that fails one cannot buy its way back with the others. Where a dimension has no evidence it is shown as n/a and never guessed: the score measures how evidenced and how well documented a party's record is, not whether its policies are good. The actual scores follow in the next section; the full algebra is in METHODOLOGY.md.

4. CEAGI - party accountability score

CEAGI = Composite Electoral Accountability & Governance Index. Six sub-indices - Delivery D (2021 promises), Promise credibility C (2021 promises), Electoral efficiency E, Governance G, Leadership L, Mandate coherence M - combined with a weighted geometric mean, so a party cannot compensate a total failure in one dimension with a strong showing in another. A dimension with no evidence is n/a; it is never imputed. A full score is published only when all six exist.

Sp = ( D0.25 · C0.20 · E0.10 · G0.20 · L0.15 · M0.10 )

9. Legal basis - what the law actually says

The seat arithmetic in the charts and the simulator comes from Morocco's organic law, not from convention. The text in force is loi organique n° 27.11 on the House of Representatives, consolidated on 29 January 2026 and amended most recently by loi organique n° 53.25 (January 2026), and before that by n° 04.21 (2021) and n° 20.16 (2016). Two articles carry the arithmetic that decides the 395 seats:

What this means in practice

  • 395 seats, two levels. 305 members are elected in local constituencies and 90 in regional constituencies; the law fixes the 90 regional seats in a table across the twelve regions.
  • Proportional, largest remainder. Seats go to lists in proportion to their support, and the seats left over after the quota go to the lists with the largest remainders.
  • The quota is based on registered voters, not votes cast. That is the distinctive Moroccan rule. In practice few lists reach the quota, so most seats are decided at the largest-remainder step.
  • No electoral threshold. The 3%/6% threshold that applied from 2002 to 2016 was removed by the 2021 reform; the current law sets none.
  • The former national list is gone. The 90 seats that used to be elected on a single national list are now elected in the regional constituencies.

The interactive seat simulator on this site applies the largest-remainder method to national vote totals that you enter. It is an illustration at national level: the real count runs constituency by constituency, on the registered-voter quotient. Read it with that in mind.

17. Methodology (short)

Sources to facts/claims to synthesis. A claim is "corroborated" only with 2+ independent reliable sources. Delivery D = (fulfilled + 0.5·partial) / (fulfilled + partial + failed + abandoned), computed over completed terms only, and requires at least 3 concluded promises before it is scored at all. Credibility C = Σ(weight·rubric) / 5Σweight with class weights Q=1.0, V=0.4, S=0.1, N=0.2. Electoral efficiency E = 1 - |A-1| / max|A-1| where A = seat share / vote share for the 2021 election. Aggregation is a weighted geometric mean; uncertainty is 8,000 Monte-Carlo draws at σ=0.05. Full derivation in morocco-elections.org; per-number provenance in output/audit_trail.md.

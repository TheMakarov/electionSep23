4. CEAGI - party accountability score

CEAGI = Composite Electoral Accountability & Governance Index. Six sub-indices - Delivery D, Claim credibility C, Electoral efficiency E, Governance G, Leadership L, Mandate coherence M - combined with a weighted geometric mean, so a party cannot compensate a total failure in one dimension with a strong showing in another. A dimension with no evidence is n/a; it is never imputed. A full score is published only when all six exist.

Sp = ( D0.25 · C0.20 · E0.10 · G0.20 · L0.15 · M0.10 )

The "provisional" column averages only the dimensions that exist. It is not comparable between parties with different coverage and is shown for diagnosis only - it is never ranked.

13. Methodology (short)

Sources to facts/claims to synthesis. A claim is "corroborated" only with 2+ independent reliable sources. Delivery D = (fulfilled + 0.5·partial) / (fulfilled + partial + failed + abandoned), computed over completed terms only, and requires at least 3 concluded promises before it is scored at all. Credibility C = Σ(weight·rubric) / 5Σweight with class weights Q=1.0, V=0.4, S=0.1, N=0.2. Electoral efficiency E = 1 - |A-1| / max|A-1| where A = seat share / vote share for the 2021 election. Aggregation is a weighted geometric mean; uncertainty is 8,000 Monte-Carlo draws at σ=0.05. Full derivation in morocco-elections.org; per-number provenance in output/audit_trail.md.

Reproducible data project. Regenerate with make check && make build. Settings in config.json. Published scores require passing the gate in section 1; until then treat every value as provisional.
